# Revenium Labs

**Revenium Labs** is where we share field-developed, best-effort solutions in the open —
working, beta-quality software we've built to solve real customer problems, but that isn't
part of our officially supported, productized offerings.

Our core products — the [Revenium SDKs, middleware, and CLIs](https://github.com/revenium) —
are versioned, documented, and formally supported. Revenium Labs projects are different by
design: they're proven starting points, not finished products.

## What "Labs" means

| | Revenium **Products** | Revenium **Labs** |
|---|---|---|
| **Maturity** | Production-ready, versioned releases | Working beta — solves a real problem today |
| **Support** | Formally supported (SLAs where applicable) | Best-effort — we'll help, no formal SLA |
| **Stability** | Semantic versioning, deprecation notices | May change; interfaces not guaranteed stable |
| **Fit** | Ready to adopt as-is | May need adaptation to your exact environment |

## What to expect from a Labs project

- **It works and it's real.** We built these to solve actual problems in the field — they're
  not throwaway experiments or demos.
- **It's provided as-is.** No versioned-release guarantees, SLAs, or formal support commitments
  that back our core products.
- **You may need to finish the last mile.** A Labs project is a strong starting point; adapting
  it to your exact stack, providers, or edge cases may be on you.
- **We want to help.** Open an issue or PR — we genuinely want your feedback, and **we're happy
  to work with you** to make a Labs project fit your use case. Or
  [come talk to us on Discord](https://discord.gg/J2DbmjZ2nA).

## How to recognize a Labs project

Every Revenium Labs repository carries:
- A **Revenium Labs** badge and a short callout at the top of its README
- The **`revenium-labs`** GitHub topic
- A `[Labs]` prefix on its repository description

If a repository has none of these, it's one of our supported products.
