# Revenium Labs

**Revenium Labs** is where we share field-developed, best-effort solutions in the open. They
are working, beta-quality software, built to solve real customer problems, but they are not
part of our officially supported products.

Our core products, the [Revenium SDKs, middleware, and CLIs](https://github.com/revenium), are
versioned, documented, and formally supported. A Labs project is different. It's a proven
starting point, and you may need to finish it for your own environment.

## What "Labs" means

| | Revenium **Products** | Revenium **Labs** |
|---|---|---|
| **Maturity** | Production-ready, versioned releases | Working beta that solves a real problem today |
| **Support** | Formally supported (SLAs where applicable) | Best-effort; we'll help, but there's no formal SLA |
| **Stability** | Semantic versioning, deprecation notices | May change; interfaces are not guaranteed stable |
| **Fit** | Ready to adopt as-is | May need adaptation to your exact environment |

## What to expect from a Labs project

- **It works and it's real.** We built these to solve actual problems in the field, not as
  throwaway experiments or demos.
- **It's provided as-is.** There are no versioned-release guarantees, SLAs, or formal support
  commitments behind it.
- **You may need to finish it for your environment.** A Labs project is a strong starting
  point, but adapting it to your exact stack, providers, or edge cases may be on you.
- **We want to help.** Open an issue or PR with your feedback, and **we're happy to work with
  you** to make a Labs project fit your use case. Or
  [come talk to us on Discord](https://discord.gg/J2DbmjZ2nA).

## Getting help and reporting problems

Labs projects do not have active product support or an SLA. For routine questions, bug
reports, and feature ideas, [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/revenium) in the relevant
repository or [talk with us on Discord](https://discord.gg/J2DbmjZ2nA).

If you discover a security vulnerability, do not report it in a public issue. Use the
repository's private GitHub security advisory process when it is available; otherwise email
[security@revenium.io](mailto:security@revenium.io).

## How to recognize a Labs project

Every Revenium Labs repository carries:
- A **Revenium Labs** badge and a short callout at the top of its README
- The **`revenium-labs`** GitHub topic
- A `[Labs]` prefix on its repository description

If a repository has none of these, it's one of our supported products.
